# Dola AI

**AI images, video, creative and office tools**

🌐 Website: [Dola AI](https://dola-ai.com/)

## About

Dola AI combines a browser creative studio with office-oriented crafts. On the creative side you can compose images, stage 3D scenes, edit video, build moodboards, extract frames and keep private projects in a library. Manual arranging and project saving do not spend generation credits. AI image, video, audio, mesh and world generation—plus AI editing such as video background work, masked redraws and character training—use credits and show a quote before submit.

Alongside ArtCraft-style creative workspaces, the product surfaces FilmCraft, PhotoCraft and office crafts such as PdfCraft, WordCraft, GridCraft and DeckCraft for documents, spreadsheets, slides and PDFs. Images and videos use the supported models listed in the studio; 3D worlds use World Labs Marble; available audio and object generation use Fal.

Pricing is credit-based: monthly Lite $29.90 (600 credits), Standard $49.90 (1,500), Pro $99.90 (3,600), Max $199.90 (8,000), yearly options at about 50% less, and one-time credit packs from Starter $39.90 (800) up through Mega. Failed jobs return reserved credits. Dola AI is for people who want visual control and light office tooling in one signed-in web account.

## Features

- Manual image editing, drawing and 3D scene composition
- Video timelines, moodboards and frame extraction
- Private saved projects and artwork
- Credit-based AI image, video, audio, mesh and world generation
- Office crafts for documents, spreadsheets, slides and PDFs
- Credit quotes before AI jobs; failed jobs return credits
